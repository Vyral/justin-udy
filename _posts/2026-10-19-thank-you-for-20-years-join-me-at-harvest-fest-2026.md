When I started in real estate 20 years ago, many of you were raising young kids. Today, some of those kids are having kids of their own, and that history deserves a real thank-you.

**Harvest Fest, my client appreciation event, is how I say it in person.** The celebration takes place Saturday, October 24, from 11 a.m. to 1 p.m. at Wheeler Farm here in Murray.

**Everyone in your circle is invited.** This invitation is open to every client I’ve worked with, your friends and family, and anyone we may work with in the future.

{% include pullquote.html %}

**Chick-fil-A lunch and treats for kids.** Lunch will be catered, and someone will be on hand to make dirty sodas. Kids can visit the balloon maker, decorate their own pumpkins, and pick whatever they like for made-to-order cotton candy. A raffle rounds out the day.

**A pumpkin pie for every family.** Even if your Saturday is packed, swing by long enough to pick up a pumpkin pie to take home. The pie gives me a reason to see you, even if it’s only for a minute or two.

**Please register at** [justinudyevents.com](https://justinudyevents.com) **before you come.** A solid headcount tells me how many pumpkin pies to order and how much Chick-fil-A to bring.

If you have any questions about the event, call me at [**801-285-0521**](tel:8012850521), email me at [**Justin@JustinUdy.com**](mailto:Justin@JustinUdy.com), or visit [**justinudy.com**](https://www.justinudy.com/). I can’t wait to see you at the farm.
